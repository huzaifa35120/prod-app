How it works

One friend, one challenge, one winner. No streaks to protect, no badges — just the two of you and a clock.

🗓️

Set the length

Pick any run from 1 to 365 days and a start date. The app builds every day of the challenge for you.

✅

Daily checkboxes

Whoever created the challenge sets the tasks for each day. You both tick your own copy, so progress is tracked per person.

🟩

Green days

Tick every task for a day and it turns green on your grid. A dot shows whether your rival finished it too.

⏱️

Productivity timer

A stopwatch per day that keeps counting if you close the app. The ring fills toward whatever your opponent has logged.

📊

Live standings

Who's ahead on focused time, days completed and tasks done — updating the moment your rival ticks something off.

🔗

Invite by link

Share a private invite code, or post the challenge publicly and let people ask for the second slot.

New in 1.1

Already have it installed? Download again and tap the file — it upgrades in place and keeps your account and history.

📝

Set the tasks up front

Build the daily checklist while you create the challenge. It gets copied onto every day, and you can still tweak an individual day later.

🔒

Results are final

When the last day passes the challenge freezes. No one can tick, untick or log time afterwards, so the result cannot be changed after the fact.

🔔

Timer in the shade

A running timer shows up as a notification with Pause, Resume and Stop, so you do not have to open the app to control it.

Installing it

Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store by default, so there's one extra tap. This is normal for any app shared directly.

  1. Tap Download for Android above. The .apk lands in your Downloads.

  2. Open it from the notification, or from Files → Downloads.

  3. Android will warn about installing unknown apps. Tap Settings, allow it for your browser, then go back.

  4. Tap Install, then Open. Create an account with any email and you're in.

⚠️

iPhone isn't supported. Apple doesn't allow apps to be installed outside the App Store, so there's no equivalent download. Android only for now.
